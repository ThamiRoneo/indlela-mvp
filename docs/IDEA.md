# Indlela - App Concept Document

## Overview

**Indlela** (isiZulu for "path" or "way") is a mobile-first application designed to tackle one of South Africa's most pressing barriers to youth employment: the rising cost of transport to seek work. 

With youth unemployment sitting at 46,1% nationally (Q1:2025) and reaching as high as 58,8% in North West, many young people between the ages of 18-35 are unable to afford the transport costs required to attend interviews, training sessions or induction days. Indlela aims to create an affordable pathway to opportunity by ensuring that transport costs do not disqualify a young job seeker from pursuing employment. 

## Target Users

- **Unemployed youth (18-35)** - Primarily first-time job seekers with little to no work experience who cannot afford repeated trips to attend interviews.
- **NEET youth** - Young people Not in Employment, Education or Training, many of whom have become discouraged due to the compounding costs of job searching.
- **SMEs and employers** - Small to medium businesses looking to hire entry-level talent, but whose candidates are often unable to attend interviews due to transport costs.
- **Job-seeking support partners** - SETAs, NGOs and initiatives such as Harambee and the Western Cape's *Getting YOU to Work* programme that have limited, quota-based transport vouchers to distribute.

## Core Features (MVP)

For its Minimum Viable Product, Indlela will focus on two features that directly combat the impact of South Africa's rising petrol price: 

1. **Dynamic Fare Index** 
   - *Crowdsourced fares:* Users can report minibus taxi fares per rank and route in real-time.
   - *Fuel-aware forecasting:* The app will leverage AA fuel price data to predict impending fare increases and alert users in advance.
   - *Offline accessibility:* Frequently used ranks and their latest fares will be cached for use with minimal data or offline, a necessity considering data costs form a large portion of a job seeker's budget. 

2. **Affordability-First Job Feed**
   - *Net-gain sorting:* Jobs will be sorted by net gain (estimated wage - estimated transport cost), as opposed to distance alone.
   - *"Jobs I can afford this week"* filter:* Allows users to filter opportunities based off of their current budget for transport.
   - *Transport-aware listings:* By integrating with the Dynamic Fare Index, estimated travel costs will be calculated using up-to-date, crowdsourced fares. 


## User Experience

1. **Onboarding:** A lightweight, data-efficient sign-up process optimised for low-end devices. 
2. **Plan their search:** Upon opening the app, a user in Rustenburg will be presented with jobs they can actually afford to get to that week, based off of current taxi fares from their nearest rank.
3. **Budget ahead:** If a fuel price increase is announced, Indlela will notify users of routes that are likely to increase in price, allowing them to prioritise affordable opportunities or budget accordingly.
4. **Apply with confidence:** With transparent transport costs upfront, users can attend interviews knowing exactly what it will cost them to get there and back, eliminating the difficult trade-off between transport, food or other necessities.
5. **Contribute:** After commuting, users can quickly report updated fares, helping keep the index accurate for the rest of their community. 

## Value Proposition

**Indlela makes job seeking affordable again.**

- **More beneficial than existing solutions:** Initiatives such as *Getting YOU to Work* and Uber/Harambee partnerships provide a fixed, short-term quota of free trips. Indlela takes a proactive approach by preventing users from wasting their limited money on unaffordable opportunities in the first place. 
- **Built for South Africa's reality:** Rather than replacing the minibus taxi industry (which accounts for the majority of commutes in townships), Indlela builds on top of it. Its rank-based, crowdsourced model is built to be sustainable even as petrol prices rise.
- **Focused impact:** Indlela will launch in **Rustenburg, North West**. North West has the highest youth unemployment rate in the country (58,8%) and the lowest level of saturation for transport-for-jobs initiatives (Q1:2025 QLFS). This allows for maximum impact with a small, bootstrappable MVP.
- **Long-term scalability:** With success in Rustenburg, Indlela can scale to other high-need areas such as the Eastern Cape, whilst providing a valuable data source on transport affordability to partners such as SETAs and policymakers. 

*Indlela doesn't just find a way to a job. It ensures that the youth of South Africa can afford to take that path.*
